# LTspice simulation

Open `ltspice/Unified_Supervisor.asc` in LTspice and click **Run** (6.4 s transient). It is one wired sheet: supply, driver, sensing, comparators, HC logic, LEDs and buzzer. The netlist LTspice generated from it was checked against all 68 intended instances; the run completed and all ten operating windows passed.

## Setup

The custom symbols and model library must be findable by LTspice.

- **Windows:** keep `Unified_Supervisor.asc`, `UnifiedSupervisor20261028.lib` and the `UnifiedSupervisor20261028/` symbol folder together in the same directory. If symbols are not found, add that folder to LTspice's symbol and library search paths.
- **macOS:** run `ltspice/Install_Mac.command` from the extracted folder, then close and reopen LTspice. It copies only this project's uniquely named library and symbols.

Do not move the `.asc` on its own.

## Test schedule

The model library function `swpos(t,k)` drives the switches; each switch's `MODE` selects its schedule: S0 closed throughout, S1 opens 1.5 to 2.2 s, S2 TEST 3.5 to 4.3 s, S3 closes 3.9 to 4.3 s, S4 command ON 0.5 to 2.5 s and 3.0 to 4.6 s, S5 forces the driver 5.0 to 5.8 s. Contacts use 10 µs ramps with a brief break-before-make interval.

## Model assumptions

- Coil: **30 Ω and 100 mH**, assumed (no measurement supplied). Edit `.param Rcoil=30 Lcoil=100m` after measuring the real coil.
- **LM339:** TI's official LM2901/LM339 family model (SLCJ010C), under the LM2901 subcircuit name.
- **LM358:** approximate ADI universal op-amp core: gain 100000, GBW 700 kHz, slew 0.3 V/µs, current limit 20 mA, 2 mV input offset, 20 nA bias. TI's newer LM358 PSpice model failed to converge in this LTspice version and is not used. The approximation does not reproduce the upper output limit, full common-mode range or temperature corners.
- **HC logic:** functional gates with 40 Ω output resistance. Fine for this slow manual sequence, not for propagation-delay or tolerance sign-off.
- IRLZ44N, 1N5408, LEDs, 2N3904, 1N4148 and 7805 are nominal engineering models. The buzzer is a 200 Ω load (about 25 mA); sound is not modeled.
- The 12 V adapter is ideal (no current limiting). The fuse is a 0.05 Ω continuity approximation and does not open.

## Known notes

- The simulated topology follows **REV F**. The hand-build schematics in `../hardware/schematics/` are **REV G**, which adds 1 kΩ / 10 kΩ protection resistors around the threshold potentiometers; those are not in the simulation. The potentiometers are modeled as ideal wiper fractions (0.170, 0.422, 0.042 giving about 0.85, 2.11 and 0.21 V).
- The LTspice log contains unrelated duplicate-model warnings from LTspice's installed BJT library and uses Gmin stepping to find the operating point. It reports successful completion.
- Temperature, tolerance corners, fuse coordination, switch arcing and short-circuit survival are not evaluated. Power-on and brownout behavior are not tested (the source starts at 12 V).
